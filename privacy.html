Privacy Policy

Anode is designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. We believe your devices, your data, and your habits belong exclusively to you.

Zero Telemetry

Anode does not collect, transmit, or store analytics, telemetry, crash dumps, or usage patterns. We do not know who you are, what files or tasks you run, or how often you use the software.

Local & Keychain Security

Any connection parameters, credentials, or encryption keys remain strictly on your local hardware within Apple's Secure Enclave and Keychain. Nothing is ever sent to third-party servers.

Direct Network Connections

When connecting to your private servers or local nodes, all traffic flows point-to-point over your local network or encrypted tunnels directly without intermediary proxies.

Contact

For questions or verification regarding this policy, reach out at hello@anode.dev.
